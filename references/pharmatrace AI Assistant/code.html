check_circle 1. Pharmacokinetic Interaction Summary

No significant cytochrome P450 competitive inhibition exists between Artemether/Lumefantrine and Metformin. Metformin is excreted unchanged through active renal tubular filtration (OCT2 pathways), while Lumefantrine undergoes extensive hepatic CYP3A4 clearance.

Spacing recommendation: In mild renal impairment (eGFR 45–59 mL/min), space Metformin 2 hours apart from the antimalarial dose to reduce transient gastrointestinal discomfort (nausea/loose stools).
restaurant Vital Dietary Fat & Bioavailability Requirement

Lumefantrine absorption is strictly lipid-dependent. Administering on a completely empty stomach yields < 15% bioavailability, risking therapeutic failure and parasitemia recrudescence.

verified Taking with whole milk, fermented milk drink (nono), or a meal containing at least 1.6g lipid elevates bioavailability to > 90%.
