Clinical Practice & Family Advice

Patient Care & Administration Guide

clinical_notes Monograph

Evidence-based clinical administration guidance for Lonart DS (Artemether / Lumefantrine) formulated for healthcare teams and primary caregivers.

Lonart DS is a fixed-dose artemisinin-based combination therapy (ACT) indicated for the complete curative treatment of acute, uncomplicated malaria infections caused by Plasmodium falciparum in adults and children weighing 35kg and above. It quickly reduces parasite biomass while Lumefantrine clears residual parasites to prevent recrudescence.
Absorption Requirement: Lumefantrine absorption is dramatically increased up to 16-fold when taken with meals containing dietary fats.
  • Always administer doses with a glass of whole milk, fortified cereal, egusi soup, or any meal prepared with cooking oils.
  • Complete the full 3-day, 6-dose regimen even if patient fever subsides within 24 hours. Early cessation breeds drug-resistant parasites.
Do not use during the first trimester of pregnancy unless advised under strict clinical discretion. Patients with pre-existing cardiac arrhythmias, baseline QT-interval prolongation, or severe electrolyte imbalances should be referred to an in-hospital doctor before starting therapy. Avoid concurrent administration of strong CYP3A4 inhibitors.

If vomiting occurs within 1 hour of ingestion, a full replacement dose must be administered immediately from a reserve packet. If the replacement dose is also vomited, contact the dispensary immediately for parenteral alternative therapy (intravenous Artesunate).

If a scheduled dose is missed, administer it as soon as remembered, then readjust the schedule while never taking a double dose at once.
