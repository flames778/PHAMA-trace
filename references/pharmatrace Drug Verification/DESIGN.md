## Brand & Style

This design system is built around warmth, intellectual calm, and deep human dignity. Rooted in the visual restraint and literary clarity of humanist editorial publishing, it creates an environment of quiet reassurance. It serves patients, families, and healthcare professionals who need clarity, psychological safety, and composure during critical moments.

The aesthetic philosophy draws on:
- **Literary Editorial Warmth:** Thoughtfully paced content reminiscent of a well-typeset book or high-end health journal, utilizing serif headlines that feel crafted rather than synthesized.
- **Warm Tactility:** Surfaces that evoke archival paper, linen, and honed limestone rather than synthetic glass or cold LCD backlights.
- **Dignified Restraint:** Zero gratuitous visual noise. No neon indicators, vibrating micro-interactions, decorative cyber grids, or synthetic glow. Every pixel serves reassurance and comprehension.
- **The Healer’s Apothecary:** A visual tone inspired by historic botanicals, unhurried consultations, and timeless institutional care—grounded, organic, and meticulously considered.

## Colors

The palette is composed of natural pigments, warm earth tones, and sunlit paper stocks.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#b44b2a` - Burnt Terracotta):** Used for focal actions, essential links, and dignified emphasis. Rooted in baked earth and wax seal signatures, it commands attention without triggering anxiety.
- **Secondary (`#8a7058` - Weathered Umber):** Applied to auxiliary markers, subtle structural indicators, and quiet metadata.
- **Tertiary (`#405b4d` - Sage Pine):** Evokes medicinal herbs and natural botanicals, serving as an organic indicator for positive confirmations, active healing states, and wellness milestones.
- **Neutral Core (`#292524` - Deep Warm Charcoal / Slate-Stone):** Replaces harsh jet black to provide gentle contrast against parchment surfaces without optical fatigue.

### Surface Tones & Architectural Canvas
- **Canvas Base:** `#faf8f5` (Warm Alabaster/Paper).
- **Surface Level 1:** `#f5f2eb` (Soft Linen).
- **Surface Level 2:** `#eeeae1` (Milled Warm Stone).
- **Delicate Outlines & Dividers:** `#e4dfd5` and `#d7d1c5`. Never use synthetic pure grays (`#e5e7eb`); always preserve the warm bone undertone.
- **Text Tiers:** Primary text rests at `#292524`, secondary metadata at `#635e59`, and muted captions at `#8c857e`.

## Typography

The type system blends the deliberate, authoritative cadence of literary serif typefaces with the optical balance of a warm humanist sans-serif.

### Pairing Logic
- **Headlines & Narrative (Newsreader):** Used for story titles, patient names, section anchors, and clinical overviews. Optical sizing cues and moderate contrast give headlines an unhurried, reassuring presence.
- **Interface & Operational Reading (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for high legibility, gentle rounded terminals, and warm proportions. Avoids mechanical rigidity while remaining crisp in multi-column data views, vitals tables, and form inputs.

### Typographic Principles
- Keep line lengths for body prose under 65 characters to encourage unhurried reading.
- Ensure all italic variants in serif display levels are reserved for reflective clinical notes, doctor quotes, or personal annotations.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a fluid, proportional grid with generous margins that honor editorial white space and avoid visual overcrowding.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid, maximum content container of `1200px`, centered with `margin: 2rem` or greater. Structural gutters sit at `1.5rem` to `2rem`.
- **Tablet (640px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer margins.

### Rhythmic Discipline
- Information density must remain comfortably aerated. Maintain wide margins around critical medical summaries to preserve cognitive ease for stressed users.
- Rely on proportional vertical stack spacing (`space-lg` and `space-xl`) to establish distinct thought boundaries rather than inserting thick dividing lines.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through subtle surface tonal steps and low-contrast warm outlines rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Tonal Stratification
- **Canvas Base (`#faf8f5`):** The foundational substrate representing unbound archival stock.
- **Resting Panels (`#f5f2eb`):** Elevated cards, prescription manifests, and conversational blocks rest directly on the canvas, bounded by a 1px border of `#e4dfd5`.
- **Active / Elevated Containers (`#ffffff`):** Modals, flyout consultation drawers, and active input fields utilize pure warm white to gently lift toward the user.

### Shadow Characteristics
- Drop shadows are used sparingly. When elevating interactive flyouts or floating contextual action sheets, use warm-tinted ambient diffusion:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -4px rgba(41, 37, 36, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(41, 37, 36, 0.03);`
- Avoid blue-gray shadows, hard directional cast shadows, or glowing edges under all circumstances.

## Shapes

The shape system employs soft, organic curvature that mirrors handcrafted ceramics, architectural arched lintels, and smoothed river stones.

- Base interactive items (buttons, inputs, select triggers) feature a `0.5rem` (8px) radius.
- Cards, patient files, and primary operational panels adopt a generous `1rem` (16px) radius (`rounded-lg`).
- Feature groupings, floating sheets, and celebratory banners scale to `1.5rem` (24px) (`rounded-xl`).
- Micro tags and status lozenges retain comfortable pill curves to contrast cleanly against structural rectangles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid terracotta background (`#b44b2a`), warm white text (`#ffffff`), `0.5rem` corner radius, `space-sm` vertical and `space-md` horizontal padding. Hover state subtly shifts toward `#9e3f22`.
- **Secondary:** Soft paper background (`#f5f2eb`), delicate border (`1px solid #d7d1c5`), text in deep slate-stone (`#292524`). Hover subtly shifts background to `#eeeae1`.
- **Tertiary / Subtle:** Transparent background, underlined text with deliberate letter spacing, transitioning to `#b44b2a` on hover.

### Input Fields
- Built on warm parchment surfaces (`#ffffff` or `#faf8f5`) with an intentional `1px solid #d7d1c5` perimeter.
- Focus state softens into a `1px solid #b44b2a` perimeter backed by an ambient glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(180, 75, 42, 0.12)`). Labels sit persistently in `Newsreader` or `Plus Jakarta Sans Medium` directly above the input with ample breathing room.

### Cards & Patient Dossiers
- Rendered in `#f5f2eb` with a delicate `1px solid #e4dfd5` boundary. 
- Headers feature `Newsreader` titles paired with lightweight metadata timestamps. Generous padding (`space-lg`) on all sides prevents cramped data presentation.

### Chips & Badges
- Avoid high-saturation pill indicators. Clinical status badges use muted botanical backgrounds:
  - Stable / Active: `#edf3ee` surface with `#405b4d` text.
  - Review Needed: `#fbf3ea` surface with `#b44b2a` text.
- Border radius remains fully rounded, with quiet, letterspaced `label-sm` typography.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Styled with organic curvature (`4px` for checkboxes, circular for radios) with a 1.5px border of `#8a7058`. Checked states fill with `#b44b2a` displaying crisp warm-white marks.

### Care Journal & Narrative Log
- A specialized vertical timeline with an archival parchment spine line (`#e4dfd5`). Entries are anchored by understated stone-colored nodes, presenting notes in graceful body type with timestamp metadata set in `label-sm`.
